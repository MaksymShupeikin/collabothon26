# comdirect edu — minimal redesign brief (for image prompts)

Product: beginner investing education platform "comdirect edu". Play money, real comdirect-like trading screen, learning cards, news-driven market simulator with bot rivals, history quiz.

Style: Robinhood calm + OKX structure. Minimal, flat, no drop shadows, no boxed cards with heavy borders, hairline dividers, lots of whitespace, big numbers, tabular figures, clean geometric sans-serif (like Gotham / Inter).

Colors (keep brand):
- Base dark: deep petrol #0B1F26 (page background in dark mode, text in light mode)
- Raised surface: #10272F, hairline divider #1C3640
- Secondary text: #83989F
- Light surface option: #FFFFFF with #DBE2E5 dividers
- Single accent: comdirect yellow #FFF500 — ONLY for the primary action button, active tab/underline, chart price line, progress
- Gain #2BD47D, loss #FF5A5F (small, never large fills)

Rules:
- One primary yellow button per screen.
- Chart = single smooth line, no grid, no axes, timeframe pills below.
- Price and name huge at top-left (Robinhood).
- Panels separated by 1px lines, not cards (OKX).
- Labels short, sentence case.
