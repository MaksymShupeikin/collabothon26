# Historical replays: fact-check notes

The replays in `src/features/market/scenarios.ts` use **real companies and real events**, retold as short headlines we wrote ourselves.
Daily OHLCV snapshots are bundled in `src/features/market/history-quotes.ts`. During playback the browser reads local data. Session dates come from the source rows. A headline's `session` identifies the first session used to show the event or its market reaction. Weekends and exchange holidays therefore do not introduce extra bars. Background commentary uses an assigned session.

- [GameStop daily OHLCV](https://github.com/djhang99/Web-Scraping-Project-1-/blob/master/GME.csv) uses contemporary USD prices.
- [NVIDIA daily OHLCV](https://github.com/Pubmarks/datasets/tree/main/data/stocks/NVDA) uses split-adjusted prices. The import restores 2023 share units by multiplying prices by ten and dividing volume by ten. Both US stocks are priced in replay EUR using that day's [Frankfurter EUR/USD reference rate](https://api.frankfurter.dev/v1/2023-05-24..2023-05-25?symbols=USD), carrying forward the previous rate on ECB holidays.
- [Volkswagen preferred shares](https://github.com/loelschlaeger/fHMM/blob/master/data/vw.rda) use EUR OHLC and the Close column.
- [DAX daily data](https://github.com/loelschlaeger/fHMM/blob/master/inst/extdata/dax.csv) uses index points divided by 110 for the scenario proxy. Its volume is the source index series' reported volume.
- [Wirecard's published daily OHLC](https://www.nebenwerte-magazin.com/tecdax-wirecard-aktie-untergang-chronologie-dieser-tage-seit-dem-18-06-2020-10-43-uhr-uebrig-bleibt/) covers June 18, 19, 22–26. Remaining sessions use the seeded model. Activity for Wirecard is simulated throughout.

The following original scenario anchors document the fallback reconstruction. Observed bars take precedence.

| Replay | Keypoints we use | What the sources say |
|---|---|---|
| **Wirecard, June 2020** (World 3 boss) | ~104,50 € on the day before the auditor news → ~40 € → ~25 € → ~14 € → ~3 € on the insolvency day → ~1,30 € | Share at 104,50 € on 17 June; EY could not confirm 1,9 bn €; on 22 June the company said the money most likely did not exist; insolvency filing 25 June, share −75–80 % that day; week ended around 1,30 €. Sources disagree slightly on dates (one says 27 June for the filing). |
| **GameStop, Jan–Feb 2021** (World 2 boss) | closes ≈ 20 $ (12 Jan) → 31 $ → 65 $ (22 Jan) → 77 $ → 148 $ (26 Jan) → 347,51 $ (27 Jan) → 193 $ → 325 $ → 225 $ (1 Feb) → 90 $ (2 Feb) → ~50 $ | Record close 347,51 $ on 27 Jan; intraday high 483 $ on 28 Jan, when trading apps restricted buying. The 2 Feb close (~90 $) is approximate. |
| **VW "Dieselgate", Sep 2015** (chapter 2.2) | 162,20 € (Fri 18 Sep) → −18,6 % → −19,8 % → +5,2 % to 111,50 € on the CEO resignation day | Same daily moves and the 111,50 € close after Winterkorn resigned on 23 Sep; intraday low 95,51 €. |
| **NVIDIA, 2023** (chapter 3.1) | ~133 € in January → +24 % on the day after the 25 May guidance → ~450 € by December | Guidance ~11 bn $ vs ~7,15 bn $ expected; share +24 % on 25 May (sources say 24–27 %); +36 % in May; "more than tripled" over 2023 is our rounding. |
| **COVID crash, Feb–Dec 2020** (World 4 boss) | DAX oil shock (−7.94% on 9 Mar), travel ban (−12.24% on 12 Mar), vaccine announcement (+4.94% on 9 Nov) | The closes before and after each of these shocks are anchored to published DAX closes. Lufthansa, Allianz, Telekom and MSCI World paths use approximate anchors. |
