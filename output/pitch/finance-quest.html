Sources and claim boundaries

Challenge brief: Challenge Card - Finance Quest, Commerzbank, Collabothon 2026, pages 1–2. Provided by the user.

Key terms: financial knowledge and action; reduce complexity; build confidence; engaging, relevant and actionable; self-directed investors; missions, challenges and real-world scenarios; goals, investment horizons, financial circumstances and experience; Innovations for all generations.

comdirect Akademie: official overview. Lessons, text, video, PDFs and interactive knowledge checks.

comdirect Musterdepot: official FAQ. Virtual portfolios, practice, help and introductory video.

eToro Academy: official FAQ and Virtual Portfolio. Education, a virtual portfolio, and platform familiarisation are documented.

Trading 212: practice mode and Learn, videos and platform navigation.

Comparison scope: An asterisk or “Not described” means that the feature is not described in the reviewed public pages. A check marks a documented feature; “Quizzes” identifies knowledge checks rather than practice trades. The table keeps eToro and Trading 212 alongside the two existing comdirect services. It does not mean competitors have no guides, videos, UI tours or in-app help. We have not audited every logged-in onboarding experience. Our demonstrated focus is guided missions in the comdirect-style trading interface.

Product hypothesis

The missing connection between learning and doing is our design opportunity. Existing services already provide useful learning and practice. We show an action-checked coach using the comdirect-style interface, rather than claiming exclusive ownership of UI guidance.

Prototype and proposed integration

The prototype contains guided lessons, UI tasks checked against user actions, missions, play money, XP, concept cards, historical scenarios and the Investor Simulator. Native bank integration and the handoff to real services are proposed. Persona examples are illustrative. Commercial effects have not been measured.

Planned: ask your own agent

MCP will expose app help and practice context to a compatible user-selected agent. MCPO is the proposed MCP-to-OpenAPI bridge for compatible OpenAPI clients. Users could ask about app screens, features and practice steps. This is planned, not implemented in the prototype.

MCPO: official project documentation

Potential pilot measures

Mission completion, comprehension, return visits and informed handoffs to existing portfolio and savings-plan services.
