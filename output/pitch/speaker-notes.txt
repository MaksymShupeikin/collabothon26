FULL SCRIPT

You know the basics. Then you open a trading app… and freeze. Courses explain the ideas, and demos let you explore. We bring them together. Meet comdirect edu: a place to learn investing by making your own decisions. We'll add MCP, so your own agent can explain the app as you practise. You see progress after every mission, giving you a reason to come back. Replay a market crash or fast-forward thirty years. See what your choices change. Starting small or planning for retirement? The examples fit your life. For Commerzbank, it’s a learning companion alongside the services customers already use. When you decide to invest real money, you’re taking steps you’ve already practised. For the bank, that first confident step can begin a relationship that lasts.

GUIDED PRACTICE LOOP
Get a hint / Try it / Get feedback / Understand why / Still unsure? Ask your agent. After understanding the result, users who still have questions can ask their own agent through the planned MCP / MCPO connection.
Users learn through actions in the interface. Each next task repeats the loop.

PLANNED AGENT SUPPORT
MCP / MCPO will let users connect a compatible personal agent and ask about app screens, features and practice steps. This capability is planned, not implemented. MCPO is an MCP-to-OpenAPI proxy: https://github.com/open-webui/mcpo
