FULL SCRIPT

Financial knowledge helps, but complexity still makes the first investment feel difficult. Others offer lessons and demos, while we add guided missions inside the comdirect trading interface. comdirect edu combines learning and practice in one simple, gamified experience. Users learn, understand, practise with play money, then apply their knowledge. Missions, feedback and rewards make learning engaging and progress easy to see. History Mode and Investor Simulator show how decisions affect financial outcomes. Different goals, budgets and horizons keep learning relevant across life stages. As a complementary bank product, it connects learning with Commerzbank's existing services. Customers practise first, so they can approach real investing with more confidence. For the bank, we aim to strengthen trust, engagement and informed first steps.
